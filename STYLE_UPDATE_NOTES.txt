Installation:
1. Make a backup copy of your existing C:\xampp\htdocs\aia_pharmacy folder.
2. Extract this ZIP.
3. Copy the included aia_pharmacy folder over the existing folder, allowing the updated files to replace the old ones.
4. Keep your existing database aia_pharmacy unchanged.
5. Open http://localhost/aia_pharmacy/ and refresh with Ctrl+F5.
